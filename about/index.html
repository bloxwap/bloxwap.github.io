About Bloxwap

Bloxwap, Inc. builds products that make trading easier to start and easier to understand.

What we make

The Bloxwap app (bloxwap.com) lets people trade crypto, stocks, gold, and indices from their phone, with Trend and Swing signals that surface opportunities for them. It is available on iOS and Android through an early-access waitlist.

Bloxwap Pro (bloxwap.pro) is the full trading terminal for experienced traders: real charts, the order book, and a complete order ticket, live in the browser.

The Bloxwap Chart SDK (@bloxwap/chart) is the charting technology behind those experiences, published as an open-source TypeScript library under the MIT license. Anyone can use it to put interactive financial charts, technical indicators, and drawing tools into their own product.

The Hyperliquid SDK (@bloxwap/hyperliquid) is Bloxwap's open-source TypeScript client for the Hyperliquid exchange API, published on npm under the MIT license.

Why this site exists

This site, bloxwap.github.io, hosts documentation for Bloxwap's open-source projects. It is maintained by Bloxwap, Inc. and published from the Bloxwap organization on GitHub, where you can read the source, report issues, and follow development.
