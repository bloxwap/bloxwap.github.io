Privacy

This notice covers bloxwap.github.io, including the Chart SDK documentation at /chart/. It is a static documentation site, and we designed it to collect as little as possible.

What this site collects

Nothing that identifies you. Bloxwap does not run analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts on this site, and it does not set cookies. There are no accounts, sign-in forms, or newsletters here.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives standard request information when you visit, such as your IP address and browser details, and may keep it in server logs for security and operations. That processing is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement, not by Bloxwap.

The chart playground

The interactive playground runs entirely in your browser with simulated market data. Your drawings and settings stay on your device: your favorite drawing tools and one display preference (whether the scroll arrows show) are saved in your browser's local storage so they are still there next time, and nothing you draw or configure is sent to Bloxwap. You can clear this data at any time by clearing site data for bloxwap.github.io in your browser.

Bloxwap products

The Bloxwap app, Bloxwap Pro, and bloxwap.com are covered by their own privacy policy and terms.
